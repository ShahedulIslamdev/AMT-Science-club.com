Wo we are

Leading Tomorrow's Science Through Knowledge & Innovation.

AMT Science Club (Astronomical Memorial of Tetra Science) is a student-led, non-profit platform dedicated to shaping a scientific, creative, and rational generation. We cross the boundaries of rote learning to spark curiosity through research, astronomy, and hands-on experiments.

Driven by our core motto — "Science for Society, Innovation for Humanity" — we empower young minds with robotics, Olympiad training, space science, and problem-solving skills to build a progressive, technology-driven future.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about the Science Club

Everything you need to know before joining — if it's not here, just reach out to us directly.

Who can join AMT Science Club?

Students from any grade or discipline who are passionate about science, astronomy, and technology are welcome to join.

What are the main objectives of AMT Science Club?

Our main goals are promoting hands-on science education, space science & astronomy, research projects, and preparing members for Science Olympiads and tech competitions

How do I participate in Olympiads?

We offer regular training sessions and mock tests to prepare our members for national and international science olympiads.

Are there any membership fees?

Basic membership is completely free! Some specific workshops or events may have nominal fees to cover material costs.

How are the decisions made within the club?

Decisions are taken democratically by a majority vote of the Executive Committee during regular monthly or emergency meetings.
